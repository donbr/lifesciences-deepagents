## PROPOSED CLINICAL COMBINATIONS FOR NF1

### **COMBINATION 1: TRIPLE THERAPY (Highest Priority)**
**Selumetinib + Dexrazoxane + Doxorubicin**

| Component | Mechanism | Status | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Doxorubicin | TOP2A targeting in tumor | Standard chemotherapy | Anti-tumor efficacy |
| Selumetinib | MEK inhibition in NF1 tumor | FDA-approved for NF1 | NF1-specific tumor targeting |
| Dexrazoxane | TOP2B inhibition in heart | FDA-approved for cardioprotection | Prevents cardiotoxicity |

**Feasibility:** HIGHEST - All components FDA-approved  
**Clinical Precedent:** None - novel combination  
**Trial Opportunity:** Phase 2 in NF1-associated MPNST

---

### **COMBINATION 2: DOSE REDUCTION STRATEGY**
**Selumetinib + Reduced-Dose Doxorubicin**

| Component | Mechanism | Dose Adjustment | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Selumetinib | MEK inhibition | Standard NF1 dose | Direct anti-tumor activity |
| Doxorubicin | TOP2A targeting | 50-75% reduction | Reduced cardiotoxicity while maintaining efficacy |

**Feasibility:** HIGH  
**Advantage:** Dose reduction is most effective cardioprotection  
**Rationale:** Selumetinib monotherapy effective in NF1 plexiform neurofibromas

---

### **COMBINATION 3: COMPREHENSIVE ANTIOXIDANT**
**Bardoxolone Methyl + NAC + Doxorubicin**

| Component | Mechanism | Status | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Doxorubicin | TOP2A targeting | Standard chemotherapy | Anti-tumor efficacy |
| Bardoxolone | NRF2 activation | Phase 3 investigational | Master antioxidant regulator |

**Advantage:** Multi-pathway antioxidant protection

### **COMBINATION 4: EPIGENETIC + ENZYMATIC TOP2B SUPPRESSION**
**Vorinostat + Dexrazoxane + Doxorubicin**

| Component | Mechanism | Level | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|-------|-----------|
| Vorinostat | HDAC inhibition | Transcriptional | Suppress TOP2B gene expression |
| Dexrazoxane | TOP2 inhibition | Enzymatic | Block remaining TOP2B activity |
| Doxorubicin | TOP2A targeting | Enzymatic | Maintain tumor efficacy |

**Feasibility:** MODERATE  
**Advantage:** Dual-level TOP2B targeting  
**Caution:** HDAC inhibitors have context-dependent effects

## DRUGS EXCLUDED FROM CONSIDERATION

| Drug | Target | Exclusion Reason | Mechanism Conflict |
|------|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Etoposide** | TOP2A/2B | TOP2 poison - INCREASES cardiotoxicity | Stabilizes TOP2-DNA complexes |
| **Mitoxantrone** | TOP2A/2B | Same cardiotoxicity mechanism as doxorubicin | Anthracenedione TOP2 poison |
| **Buthionine sulfoximine** | Glutathione synthesis | Depletes protective GSH pathway | Inhibits γ-GCS enzyme |
| **Letrozole/Anastrozole/Exemestane** | Estrogen synthesis | Eliminates cardioprotective estrogen | Aromatase inhibition |
