### **🛡️ Cardioprotection Genes/Pathways (Minimize Toxicity)**

#### **1. TOP2B (Topoisomerase IIβ) - HIGH CONFIDENCE** ⭐
- **Mechanism**: Primary mediator of doxorubicin cardiotoxicity in non-dividing cardiomyocytes
- **Therapeutic Strategy**: **Dexrazoxane** (FDA-approved) - dual mechanism:
  - Iron chelation (prevents reactive oxygen species)
  - TOP2B inhibition (prevents DNA damage in heart tissue)
- **Clinical Evidence**: 80% reduction in cardiac events; only FDA-approved cardioprotectant for anthracyclines
- **Gene**: HGNC:11990, UniProt:Q02880, Chr 3p24.2

#### **2. CBR1 & CBR3 (Carbonyl Reductases) - HIGH CONFIDENCE** ⭐
- **Mechanism**: Convert doxorubicin to **doxorubicinol** (13-fold more cardiotoxic metabolite)
- **Therapeutic Strategy**: CBR1 inhibitors (investigational)
  - **Curcumin** shows CBR1 inhibitory activity
  - Genetic variants associate with cardiotoxicity risk
- **Pathway**: NRF2 oxidative stress response
- **Genes**: CBR1 (HGNC:1548, Chr 21q22.12), CBR3 (HGNC:1549, Chr 21q22.12)

#### **3. RARG (Retinoic Acid Receptor Gamma) - MEDIUM CONFIDENCE**
- **Mechanism**: Nuclear receptor activating cardioprotective gene programs
- **Therapeutic Strategy**: **All-trans retinoic acid (ATRA)** supplementation
- **Evidence**: Preclinical studies support cardioprotection; clinical trials ongoing
- **Pathway**: Vitamin A metabolism, lipid metabolism & toxicity pathways
- **Gene**: HGNC:9866, UniProt:P13631, Chr 12q13.13

#### **4. Drug Efflux Transporters - MEDIUM CONFIDENCE**
- **ABCB1** (P-glycoprotein): Protects normal tissues from doxorubicin accumulation
