# Doxorubicin Toxicity: Genes and Pathways Minimizing Cardiotoxicity While Preserving Anti-Tumor Efficacy

## Summary

Doxorubicin (CHEMBL:53463 / PubChem:CID31703) exerts its anti-tumor effect by inhibiting DNA topoisomerase II alpha (TOP2A, HGNC:11989), which is enriched in dividing tumor cells. Its dose-limiting cardiotoxicity is mediated through a distinct isoform, DNA topoisomerase II beta (TOP2B, HGNC:11990), which is expressed in post-mitotic cardiomyocytes. This isoform-specific toxicity opens opportunities for selective cardioprotection. Three interconnected gene/pathway axes minimize toxicity while preserving efficacy: (1) the TOP2B axis, where dexrazoxane (CHEMBL:1738) achieves cardioprotection by degrading TOP2B; (2) the NRF2/KEAP1 antioxidant response pathway, which induces cytoprotective enzymes (NQO1, HMOX1, SOD2); and (3) the ROS detoxification pathway, which neutralizes superoxide radicals generated by doxorubicin redox cycling. Seven clinical trials (NCT03930680, NCT06219032, NCT05271162, NCT07370506, NCT06155331, NCT06427226, NCT06092606) are testing cardioprotective strategies spanning TOP2B degradation, SGLT2 inhibition, ARB therapy, and PPARa agonism. [Source: Open Targets GraphQL knownDrugs(ENSG00000131747), opentargets_get_associations(ENSG00000077097), clinicaltrials_search_trials("doxorubicin cardiotoxicity")]

## Cardioprotective Gene Network

### Axis 1: NRF2/KEAP1 -- Master Antioxidant Response Pathway

NFE2L2 (HGNC:7782, UniProtKB:Q16236), also known as NRF2, is a bZIP transcription factor that binds antioxidant response elements (ARE) in the promoter regions of cytoprotective genes. Under basal conditions, NRF2 is ubiquitinated and degraded by the BCR(KEAP1) complex. KEAP1 (HGNC:23177, UniProtKB:Q14145) acts as a sensor for oxidative stress -- electrophilic modification of its cysteine residues disrupts the KEAP1-NRF2 interaction, allowing NRF2 nuclear translocation and transcriptional activation. [Source: uniprot_get_protein(UniProtKB:Q16236)]

**NRF2 Pathway Membership (WP:WP3 -- Transcriptional activation by NRF2):**

Members from the knowledge graph: NFE2L2, KEAP1, HMOX1, NQO1. Additional pathway components include GCLC, GCLM, GSTA2, SLC7A11, MAF, PIK3CA. [Source: wikipathways_get_pathway_components(WP:WP3)]

**NRF2-Doxorubicin Relevance:**

NQO1 is especially relevant to doxorubicin cardioprotection. Doxorubicin is a quinone that undergoes redox cycling via one-electron reduction, generating toxic superoxide radicals. NQO1 catalyzes an alternative two-electron reduction pathway that bypasses superoxide generation, directly producing the less toxic hydroquinone. [Source: wikipathways_get_pathway_components(WP:WP3)]

### Axis 2: ROS Detoxification Pathway (WP:WP2824)

SOD2 (HGNC:11180, UniProtKB:P04179), also known as MnSOD, destroys superoxide anion radicals normally produced within mitochondria, protecting cardiomyocytes from oxidative damage generated by doxorubicin redox cycling. [Source: uniprot_get_protein(UniProtKB:P04179)]

**ROS Detoxification Pathway Members (WP:WP2824 -- Detoxification of reactive oxygen species):**

SOD1/2/3, CAT, GPX1-8, PRDX1-3/6, TXN, TXN2, TXNRD1/2, GSTP1, NOX4/5. [Source: wikipathways_get_pathway_components(WP:WP2824)]

### Axis 3: Drug Transport -- ABCB1

ABCB1 (HGNC:40, UniProtKB:P08183), also known as MDR1 or P-glycoprotein, is an ATP-dependent efflux pump that transports doxorubicin out of cells. ABCB1 determines doxorubicin tissue distribution and intracellular accumulation. In cardiomyocytes, ABCB1-mediated efflux is cardioprotective. In tumor cells, however, high ABCB1 expression confers multidrug resistance, presenting a therapeutic window challenge. [Source: hgnc_get_gene(HGNC:40)]

## Cardioprotective Drug Candidates

### Dexrazoxane (CHEMBL:1738)

Dexrazoxane is the only FDA-approved cardioprotectant for anthracycline-induced cardiotoxicity. It acts as a DNA topoisomerase II inhibitor on TOP2B (Phase 4), achieving cardioprotection by degrading TOP2B protein in cardiomyocytes. Trial NCT03930680 (COMPLETED) demonstrated that dexrazoxane achieves 95% reduction in TOP2B within 8 hours. [Source: Open Targets GraphQL knownDrugs(ENSG00000077097), clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:03930680)]

**Mechanism chain:**
```
Dexrazoxane --[TOP2B degradation]--> 95% reduction in TOP2B protein
                                        |
                                        +--> Doxorubicin cannot form TOP2B-DNA cleavage complexes
                                        |
                                        +--> Preserved TOP2A in tumor cells --> Anti-tumor efficacy maintained
```

### Trial Insights

**Completed Evidence (NCT03930680, NCT06155331):**
Two completed trials validate distinct cardioprotective strategies. NCT03930680 confirmed dexrazoxane's mechanism of action (95% TOP2B reduction within 8 hours), providing L4 clinical evidence for TOP2B-targeted cardioprotection. NCT06155331 tested fenofibrate (PPARa agonism) as a prophylactic strategy. [Source: clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:03930680), clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:06155331)]

**Recruiting Trials (NCT06219032, NCT05271162):**
NCT06219032 extends dexrazoxane use to DLBCL patients receiving R-CHOP (which includes doxorubicin). NCT05271162 (EMPACT) tests empagliflozin, an SGLT2 inhibitor with demonstrated cardiovascular benefits in heart failure, as a novel cardioprotective strategy during anthracycline therapy. [Source: clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:06219032), clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:05271162)]

**Emerging Strategies (NCT07370506, NCT06427226, NCT06092606):**
Three trials in early stages test telmisartan (ARB), dapagliflozin (SGLT2i), and DH001 (novel agent), reflecting a broadening of cardioprotective approaches beyond TOP2B-specific mechanisms to include cardiovascular drugs repurposed for anthracycline cardioprotection. [Source: clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:07370506), clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:06427226), clinicaltrials_get_trial(NCT:06092606)]
