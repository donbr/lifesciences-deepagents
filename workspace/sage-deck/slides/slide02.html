What We Heard

No Baseline Exists

There is no standardized process for comparing LLM tool outputs in life sciences. Teams evaluate tools ad hoc, making procurement and adoption decisions on anecdote rather than evidence.

Use Cases Must Be Persona-Driven

Different researchers need different things. A trainee learning pathway biology has fundamentally different needs than a PI orchestrating a multi-site collaboration.

Structured Testing Matters

Sage teams want reproducible test cases with known answers — not cherry-picked demos. Gold-standard questions with verifiable paths are the currency of trust.

Language Needs Translation

Technical capabilities must be expressed in terms researchers actually use: "Can it find drugs for my target?" not "Does it resolve HGNC IDs?"
