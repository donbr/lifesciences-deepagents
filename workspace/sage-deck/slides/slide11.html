Evidence Grading: L1-L4 Pyramid

L4 — Clinical Evidence (0.90-1.00)

Drug-target confirmed by trials or FDA approval

L3 — Functional Evidence (0.70-0.89)

Validated by experimental data (STRING, BioGRID)

L2 — Multi-Database (0.50-0.69)

Confirmed across 2+ databases

L1 — Single Database (0.30-0.49)

Found in one source only

Synapse Grounding Upgrade

When a KG node or edge is grounded in a Synapse dataset, its evidence level can be upgraded.

Example:

NRF2/KEAP1 at L2 → grounded by syn205590 (Nrf2 KO mouse) → upgradeable to L3
